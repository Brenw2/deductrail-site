Delete your Deductrail account

This page explains how to delete your account and data in Deductrail, the mileage and expense tracking app from Wade Works LLC.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Deductrail and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Under Your data, tap Delete account and data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
  5. Tap Delete account and data.

Settings›Your data›Delete account and data

If you want a copy of your data, export it first: Settings › Your data › Export all data (CSV).

If you use Deductrail without signing in, your data is stored only on your device, and the same steps erase it from that device.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Email privacy@deductrail.com from the email address on your Deductrail account, with the subject Delete my account. We use that address to find your account.

What is deleted, and when

Deleting your account removes, immediately and permanently:

  • your account
  • your trips
  • your expenses
  • your businesses
  • your receipts, including receipt photos

This removes the data from our servers and from the device you delete from. It can’t be undone.

If you delete an individual trip, expense, or business without deleting your account, it is kept on our servers for 30 days so your other devices can sync the change, and then it is permanently purged. A receipt photo is removed as soon as its expense is deleted.

What is kept

After your account is deleted, we keep none of your account data, except as our privacy policy states.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Deductrail Pro subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.
